## Long description

### Build the fort. Then fight in it.

CombatForge is a multiplayer airsoft shooter built around one idea: **the arena isn't handed to you —
you make it.**

Every match starts with a build phase. You and your squad have two and a half minutes and a limited
budget to turn an empty warehouse into something defensible. Walls, floors, ramps, roofs. Doorways and
window frames to shoot through. One-way doors that only open your way. Trap floors for anyone who
takes the obvious route. A tinted screen splits the arena down the middle while you work, so neither
side sees what the other is building until it clears.

Then the wall drops and you fight through it.

### The forts don't reset

Most shooters give you the same map forever. CombatForge runs a **three-match rotation**:

1. **Creative** — build from nothing.
2. **Remix** — the map you just fought in comes back. Rebuild it, reinforce what failed, tear out what
   didn't work.
3. **Remix Swap** — the same arena, but **the squads switch sides**. Now you're defending the fort
   your enemy built, and every murder-hole they cut is one you have to live with.

That third match is the one that sells the game. Nobody builds the same way twice once they know
they'll have to defend it.

### Non-lethal by design

CombatForge is airsoft, not war. Shots are marked hits, not kills — players are **out**, not dead.
The guns are realistic, the tactics are real, and nobody bleeds. It's the tension of a tactical
shooter without the gore, which makes it something you can hand to a friend, a squadmate, or your
kid.

### What's in it

- **Build system** — walls, floors, ramps, roofs, doors, windows, one-way doors, trap floors, and
  cover props, all placed on a grid with a per-player budget and a radial quick-select wheel.
- **35+ weapons** across rifles, SMGs, shotguns, snipers, LMGs and pistols, unlocked by rank.
- **Five classes**, each with its own loadout and role.
- **Game modes** — Skirmish, Elimination, Domination, Hardpoint, Capture the Flag, and Free-for-All.
- **Community maps** — save the arena you built, vote on other people's, and remix the best ones.
- **Dedicated servers** with an in-game browser, plus LAN and listen-host play.
- **Bots** to fill out a roster when you're short.
- **Full crossplay** — everyone plays together regardless of where they got the game.

### Beta

CombatForge is in active development and this is a **beta**. Expect rough edges, expect balance
changes, and expect progression to be reset from time to time. The build-and-fight loop is solid and
playable right now — the polish is still coming.

If you find a bug, the in-game reporter goes straight to the team.
